# **GAME DESIGN:**

- **CORE GAMELOOP: Combat, weapons drops, loot, dungeon explore, GOAL: kill demon king**
- **World inspired by Terraria**
    - World gets generated once using procedural noise techniques *multiple passes* and saved to file
    - Hand drawn structures are placed via procedural generation
    - 3 Major continents (Human, elf & Demon) each level rising with difficulty
    - ALL three contients are seperate islands you travel from one to another after you've defeated the man dungeon master
    - There's a boat at the edge of every continent which would travel to another continent, but the captian refuses
      to travel he says somthing like: "Due to the main dungeon appearing in the country, no one is allowed to leave, i cannot set sail"
    - Once the main dungeon master has been defeated player can travel to elf country, the progression is somewhat linear from Human -> Elf -> Demon *for now*
    - The demon continent is slowly spreading like curruption in terraia
    - Every continent's every village has dungeons spawning that are emerging because of the demon continent
      wanting to cover the whole world in dungeons and ash instead of grass and demonic atmosphere where low
      level demons can spawn even on the surface
    - The demons want to cover the entire world hence they are spawning dungeons near every village
    - Ash which spawn demons spreads from dungeons, the spread slows down every time a dungeon level is cleared
    - Once the dugneon boss is defeated the ash slowly disappears and nature takes back over
    - Once a dungeon is cleared by the player i will slowly turn into nature and grass will come back *No longer curruped*
    - Players end goal is to clear out all the dungeons
    - Once all the dungeons are cleared the final dungeon for end boss will appear with 10 custom handmade detailed levels *Kinda like minecraft end portal area*
    - Around 50 weapons
        - Only 5 to 10 different weapon classes
        - Rest approx 40 Weapons have special effects like burn, bleed
    - Each dungeon boss has a 100% guranted drop of either a weapon or accesory item
    - The more difficult the boss/enemy the better modifier will the weapon have
- **WorldMap inspired by CurseMark** 
    - The demon continent has lava around it
- **Art direction**
    - 2d topdown pixel art style
    - No outline characters and items (for eg: *Terrafactor* by Randy)
    - Tilemap tiles blend smoothly (for eg: *Tangy TD* by cakez's game )
